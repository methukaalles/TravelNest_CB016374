Frequently Asked Questions

How do I save a trip?

Click Save on any generated trip or budget plan. Everything is stored in your browser.

Is my data private?

Yes — all data is saved locally in your browser using localStorage.

Can I use this offline?

Yes! The site is a Progressive Web App (PWA) and works offline after first visit.
